# ROI Comparison Chart - Requirements

## Image File
**Target**: `roi-comparison.png`

## Usage
**Location**: Business Value section
**Purpose**: Show time/cost savings

## Visual Requirements

### Layout
- **Type**: Bar chart or comparison chart
- **Orientation**: Horizontal or vertical bars
- **Style**: Professional chart with clear labels

### Data to Show

#### 1. Time per Merge Request
- **Before**: 30 minutes
- **After**: 1 minute
- **Savings**: 29 minutes (96.7% reduction)
- **Visual**: Side-by-side bars
- **Color Before**: Red/Orange (`#ff6b6b`)
- **Color After**: Green (`#51cf66`)

#### 2. Infrastructure Costs
- **Before**: High cost (visual representation)
- **After**: Low cost (visual representation)
- **Savings**: 60-80% reduction
- **Visual**: Side-by-side bars or cost indicators
- **Color Before**: Red (`#ff6b6b`)
- **Color After**: Green (`#51cf66`)

#### 3. Developer Hours Saved per Month
- **Metric**: Hours saved per developer
- **Value**: ~20 hours per developer per month
- **Visual**: Bar or number display
- **Color**: Green (`#51cf66`)

### Chart Elements
- **Title**: "ROI Comparison" or "Time & Cost Savings"
- **Axes**: Clear labels (Time, Cost, Hours)
- **Legend**: Before/After labels
- **Values**: Numbers clearly displayed
- **Percentage**: Show percentage savings

### Visual Style
- **Background**: Light or dark (depending on presentation theme)
- **Bars**: Rounded or sharp corners
- **Labels**: Clear, readable text
- **Colors**: 
  - Before: Red/Orange (`#ff6b6b`, `#ffa500`)
  - After: Green (`#51cf66`)
  - Neutral: Gray for labels

### Additional Elements
- **Savings Indicators**: Percentage badges
- **Icons**: Clock icons for time, dollar signs for cost
- **Callouts**: Highlight key savings
- **Trend Arrows**: Down arrows for reductions

## Technical Specifications
- **Format**: PNG
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (16:9 aspect ratio)
- **File Size**: < 2MB
- **Background**: Light or dark (match presentation)

## Design Notes
- Professional chart appearance
- Clear visual contrast (red vs green)
- Easy to read numbers and labels
- Emphasize savings with green
- Enterprise-grade presentation quality
